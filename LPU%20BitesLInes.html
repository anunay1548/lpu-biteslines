A simple campus problem

Large campuses make small errands inconvenient. CampusCarry turns students' existing movement into a helpful delivery network.

🏫

Large campus

Hostels can be 1–1.5 km away from food courts, shops and other facilities near the main gate.

🚶

Students are already moving

Many students are already going toward N.K Food Court and Food Court 2. They can carry an item for someone else.

💰

Everyone benefits

The requester saves time, the helper earns a service fee, and local campus businesses can get more orders.

How CampusCarry works

One request, one nearby helper, one simple delivery.

1

Create request

Tell us what you need and where it should be picked up.

2

Find a helper

Students near that location see the request and can accept it.

3

Pick up & deliver

The helper buys or collects the item and brings it to the hostel.

4

Confirm & earn

Delivery is confirmed with an OTP and the helper receives the service fee.

Core features

The first version should stay simple, useful and easy for students to understand.

📍

Nearby matching

Show requests relevant to students near N.K Food Court and Food Court 2.

💬

Chat

Requester and helper can clarify the item and delivery details.

🔐

Student verification

Use university registration details or approved student authentication.

⭐

Ratings

Build trust with ratings and completed-delivery history.

🔢

Delivery OTP

A short confirmation code helps prevent delivery disputes.

💳

Service fee

Keep item cost separate from the helper's service charge.

🚶

I'm going there

Helpers can announce that they are already travelling toward a location.

📊

History & earnings

Students can review previous requests, deliveries and earnings.
